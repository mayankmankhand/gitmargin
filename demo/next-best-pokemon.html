A GITMARGIN DEMO

Jeff has bugs. Catch them.

Jeff is an AI that picks your first Pokémon. His prototype has 5 wild bugs. Comment on each one, the way you would in a Google Doc.

  1. Press Comment in the bar at the top right.
  2. Click anything that looks off and say what you expected.
  3. Press the count badge to see your comments, grouped by screen. Copy for author shows what a coding agent would get.

Your comments stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. Easiest on a laptop.

Stuck? Show the 5 bugs
  1. Hello there: "Trust my instinct" ignores your instinct.
  2. Question 2: it says "2 of 3". There is no question 3.
  3. Jeff's pick: a match confidence of 112%.
  4. Confirm: the type is wrong.
  5. Done: "Congradulations".

gitmargin is free and open source: github.com/mayankmankhand/gitmargin
